### Share an app

If you run a church, classroom, coaching practice or newsletter, the [partner kit](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/partners/) has ready-to-paste blurbs, badges, QR codes and a tagged link for every app. Sharing is free and optional, and nobody is paid for it.

### How we work

- Most apps need no account, and the free resources never ask for one.
- No invented reviews, counts or countdowns. If something is limited, the real date is shown.
- Printables, data and infographics are free to use; each page states its licence.
